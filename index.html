About us

Chacha Chachi Tours and Travels was started by a husband and wife, Ramprashad Lamsal and Usha Lamsal. For ten years they have carried families, students, workers and pilgrims between Butwal and Delhi, and passengers have come to call them Chacha and Chachi, uncle and aunt.

We own our bus and run our own offices at both ends, so you always speak to someone who knows your journey. That is why we are one of the most recommended travels on this route.
